One family, many voices

Body · 400 · 1.6

Body copy is set at 16px with a 1.6 line height and a measure capped at 64 characters. Links carry a hairline underline that tightens on hover, and nothing else moves. Captions and metadata step down in size and colour, not in family.

Whitespace is the most expensive material on the page, so spend it deliberately.
